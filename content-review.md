# Visionary Innovations content review

This document separates source material from public-facing copy. The two decks are working materials and contain claims that require written approval or supporting evidence.

## Approved-looking public content used in the draft

- Visionary Innovations is developing geothermal energy and water-desalination concepts.
- The source materials describe two pathways: a volcanic geothermal heat-transfer concept and an enclosed-loop or multimode geothermal concept.
- The proposed volcanic pathway uses heat-retaining spheres and specialized piping as a heat-transfer premise.
- Potential applications include electricity generation, thermal desalination, industrial heat, and other downstream uses that require separate validation.
- Coastal, island, volcanic, and geothermal regions are relevant contexts for research and site screening.
- Investor and licensing inquiries should be reviewed before sharing confidential materials.

## Claims requiring founder confirmation or supporting evidence

The following claims from the decks are intentionally excluded from the public page:

- “World’s first,” “only system,” “no direct competitor,” or any market-leadership claim.
- Commercial performance, cost savings, environmental footprint, efficiency, or operating reliability.
- Specific temperatures, materials, material suitability, durability, or safety performance.
- Patent, utility-patent, or PCT filing status.
- Government, ministry, utility, university, strategic buyer, or advertiser interest.
- Market-size figures, CAGR, projected revenue, investor returns, funding terms, ownership percentages, valuation, or exit timing.
- Named projects, operating plants, pilots, project budgets, PPAs, water contracts, or commercial timelines.
- Any statement that the two pathways already operate as one integrated product.

## Missing information before publication

- Final legal company name, incorporation jurisdiction, and approved public address.
- Approved company email, phone number, privacy notice, and inquiry recipient.
- Founder and team biographies, titles, headshots, and consent to publish.
- Current technical status: concept, prototype, test results, or third-party validation.
- Technical review of the heat-transfer, piping, sphere, power-generation, and desalination descriptions.
- Environmental, safety, permitting, and site-selection review.
- Written rights or licenses for every reused image and graphic.
- Approved form processor, anti-spam method, retention policy, and data-handling notice.

## Questions for the founders

1. Which exact company name, contact details, and legal disclaimers can appear publicly?
2. Are the two concepts separate products, a planned platform, or only adjacent ideas at this stage?
3. What technical evidence may be described publicly, and which details must remain confidential?
4. Which countries or regions should be discussed, if any, after verification?
5. What inquiry endpoint and recipient should handle investment and licensing submissions?
6. Which supplied visuals are cleared for web use, and what attribution or license language is required?

## Public-site posture

The current site is a restrained, non-promotional concept overview. It does not publish the investment deck, financial projections, investment terms, private contact details, unverified partnerships, or unsupported technical claims.
